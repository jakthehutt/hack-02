## What it was cited for

The Austrian national component of ParlaMint, encoded and supplied by ACDH-CH. It holds Austrian parliamentary records for 1996 to 2022, TEI-encoded and enriched with additional metadata.

Cited for the coverage window. The 2022 end date is the practical constraint on our design: the AIES study period and anything current fall outside it, so recent sessions must be pulled from the Austrian Parliament's own open data and reconciled with the ParlaMint schema. Whether a later ParlaMint release extends the Austrian end date past 2022 is unresolved and flagged as an open item.
