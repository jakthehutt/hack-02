# Russian propaganda exploiting French wildfires to discredit support for Ukraine

- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-26

## What it was cited for

Documents how the genuine French wildfire emergency of summer 2026 was used to carry invented content, notably fabricated petitions presented as evidence that French citizens had turned against their country's support for Ukraine. The report states that the fake material first surfaced on Russian channels operating on the MAX messaging platform and was then amplified by the Pravda network and pro-Kremlin Telegram channels.

Cited for case example three, and specifically for the origin-layer detail: the first observable appearance was on a Russian-controlled messenger, not on a public news site. Ukrinform is a state news agency of a belligerent party, so the origin-platform claim in particular should be corroborated before it appears in a publication.
