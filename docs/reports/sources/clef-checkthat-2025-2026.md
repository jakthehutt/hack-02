## What it was cited for

The 2025 edition of this fact-checking evaluation lab ran tasks on subjectivity identification, claim normalization, fact-checking numerical claims, and scientific web discourse. The claim normalization task is the directly relevant one: it evaluates turning informal social-media posts into concise, self-contained, verifiable statements that preserve factual content while stripping opinion, redundancy and extraneous material, and its monolingual setting covers thirteen languages including German. The 2026 edition shifts toward source retrieval for scientific web claims, numerical and temporal fact-checking with an explicit reasoning component, and generation of complete fact-checking articles.

Cited as the established formalism and German-language precedent for the normalisation step in our claim register, which is what makes a claim comparable across a Telegram post, a laundered news article and a parliamentary speech.
