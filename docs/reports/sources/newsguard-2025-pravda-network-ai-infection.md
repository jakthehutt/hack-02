## What it was cited for

The report that popularised the term LLM grooming. It describes the Pravda network as a Moscow-based operation running roughly 182 domains across 74 countries and publishing on the order of 155 articles per day, using search-engine optimisation to raise the visibility of its content so that systems relying on search-indexed material are more likely to draw on it. In an audit of ten prominent chatbots, NewsGuard reports the network's narratives were repeated about a third of the time, with Pravda sites cited as if they were legitimate outlets. Pravda is presented as a continuation of the larger Portal Kombat network that VIGINUM identified in February 2024 and dated to February 2022.

Cited for the network's scale and its crawler-facing design, which is what our collection layer has to defend against. The one-third figure is disputed — other researchers report rates around five percent and argue the concern outruns the evidence — so the audit result must be labelled as contested wherever it appears. The structural facts about domain count and SEO strategy are the parts our design actually depends on.
