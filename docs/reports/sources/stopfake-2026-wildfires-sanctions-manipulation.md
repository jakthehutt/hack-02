## What it was cited for

A debunk of the claim that the severity of the 2026 wildfires in France and Spain was caused by European sanctions on Russia, specifically sanctions affecting Russian firefighting-equipment manufacturers. The analysis describes the claim's spread as bearing the marks of a coordinated operation amplified by pro-Russian politicians, with the aim of presenting sanctions as costing European lives and property. Independent reporting attributes the fires themselves to record heat and drought, with June 2026 the hottest June on record in Western Europe according to the Copernicus Climate Change Service.
