# Russian 'Disinformation' Hyped Paris Bedbug Scare, French Deputy Minister Says

- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-26

## What it was cited for

Reports French minister Jean-Noël Barrot stating in March 2024 that the international bedbug panic around Paris was to a large extent amplified by social-media accounts linked to the Kremlin. The underlying infestation was genuine and began in Paris rentals and hotels in the second half of 2023 before spreading to cinemas and public transport. Coverage of the episode also records that the amplification effort attached a false frame linking the infestation to the arrival of Ukrainian refugees, with the apparent goal of manufacturing a sense of insecurity.

Cited as case example one, and as evidence for the general pattern: the event was real and locally produced, while the causal story attached to it was the imported element. Note that the attribution rests on a government minister's public statement rather than a published technical analysis.
