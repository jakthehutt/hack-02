## What it was cited for

Reports analyst findings on the information dynamics of the Ceuta crisis. The propaganda push is traced to Rybar, a Telegram channel reported to have links to Russian intelligence, whose posts were then picked up and spread by a network of repeater accounts including accounts tied to the Pravda network. Related coverage records more than 1,500 items published across the pro-Russian network led by Pravda and RT, with close to 300 in its Spanish-language edition, and a European Commission statement dating the rapid cross-platform amplification from 30 July.

This is the source that establishes the layered path our concept's layer model is built on: a Telegram origin channel, a laundering layer of networked domains and repeater accounts, then domestic far-right amplification. It is the clearest published example of the sequence we intend to measure.
