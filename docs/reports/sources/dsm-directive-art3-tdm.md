## What it was cited for

The EU Digital Single Market copyright directive, adopted 17 April 2019, created two text-and-data-mining exceptions, one specific to scientific research and a narrower one available more generally. Article 3 permits reproductions and extractions made by research organisations and cultural heritage institutions, for the purposes of scientific research, from works or other material to which they have lawful access. Article 2(2) defines text and data mining broadly, as any automated analytical technique applied to digital text and data in order to generate information, including patterns, trends and correlations, and member states are required to provide the exception.

Cited as the legal basis our news-collection component should state in its provenance records. Two conditions do real work and must be satisfied rather than assumed: the collector has to qualify as a research organisation, which is where the institutional affiliation matters, and access to the material has to be lawful in the first place.
