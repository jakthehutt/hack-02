## What it was cited for

A Gartner assessment of the narrative-intelligence vendor category. Blackbird.AI is reported as positioned highest for both Potential to Execute and Potential for Market Disruption in the June 2026 Emerging Market Quadrant for Narrative Intelligence, and is named the company to beat in the disinformation segment.

Cited to establish that a commercial market exists and is being formally tracked as a category, which matters for how we position the project: the contribution is not that nobody sells narrative tracking, it is that none of what is sold provides a citable provenance chain, a documented estimator, or published error rates at a research-relevant prevalence. The document itself is behind Gartner's paywall; the positioning claims were retrieved from the abstract and from vendor citation of it, which is a weaker evidentiary basis than reading the report.
