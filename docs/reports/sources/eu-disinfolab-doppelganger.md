## What it was cited for

The reference resource on the Doppelgänger operation, which places pro-Kremlin material on counterfeit websites that impersonate established news brands by registering lookalike domains and copying layout and style, with the falsified articles then pushed by bot-like social accounts. Reporting attributes the campaign to the Russian IT firm Social Design Agency and dates it to 2022. German media brands whose domains have been cybersquatted include Der Spiegel, Neues Deutschland, Bild, T-Online, Die Welt, Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung, Der Tagesspiegel and Süddeutsche Zeitung, and the operation has also been observed running invented news brands of its own.

Cited because it defeats the most common shortcut in news collection: trusting the domain name as a proxy for the publisher. A collector that records content by domain will attribute Kremlin material to legitimate German outlets, which would corrupt our source counts in exactly the direction that inflates apparent spread.
