A weekly fact-checking review covering the Ceuta migration crisis of late July and early August 2026. It documents specific fabricated claims circulating in the pro-Kremlin ecosystem, including stories from the Pravda network asserting that Spain's government had abandoned the city and old photographs repurposed to suggest Spanish ministers were celebrating at the Moroccan embassy during a supposed new Green March against Spain.

Cited for the claim-level detail of case example two: the specific frames, not just the topic. Detector Media is a Ukrainian media-monitoring NGO, so it is an interested party in this conflict; the same claims are corroborated by the Spanish government's public statements and by independent analyst reporting.
