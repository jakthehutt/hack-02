## What it was cited for

From 2 March 2022, as part of the fourth sanctions package, the EU suspended the broadcasting activities of RT and Sputnik. The regulation prohibits operators from broadcasting, or from enabling, facilitating or otherwise contributing to the broadcast of, content from the listed outlets by any means, including cable and satellite transmission or distribution. The suspension is framed as lasting until Russia ends its aggression against Ukraine and its media stop conducting propaganda against the EU and its member states.

Cited because the original concept proposed using RT directly as the primary upstream source. Two consequences follow. Practically, EU-side blocking makes those domains unreliable to fetch and mirrors become the observable, which points at the laundering layer as the better measurement point anyway. Legally, whether research analysis and archiving fall outside broadcasting and distribution is not something the retrieved sources settle, and it is recorded as an open question requiring a qualified legal opinion rather than an inference of ours.
