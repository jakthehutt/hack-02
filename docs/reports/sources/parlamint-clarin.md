## What it was cited for

The repository entry for the ParlaMint corpus family, a CLARIN flagship effort covering parliamentary debate for 29 European countries and autonomous regions from at least 2015 to 2022, totalling over a billion words. Records are uniformly encoded in TEI, carry structured metadata for roughly 24,000 speakers, and are distributed both as plain text with TSV speech metadata and as linguistically annotated versions reaching Universal Dependencies syntax and named entities, with CoNLL-U output available. Corpora can also be queried online through CLARIN.SI concordance tools.

Cited to establish that the downstream measurement layer for our pipeline already exists in annotated form, so the project should consume it rather than re-derive speaker metadata and linguistic annotation.
