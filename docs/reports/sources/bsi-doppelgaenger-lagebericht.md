# Doppelganger disinformation campaign (situation report)

- **URL:** https://medien.bsi.bund.de/lagebericht/en/doppelgaenger-desinformationskampagne/
- **Publisher / venue:** Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI), Germany
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-26
- **Cited in:** 2026-09-26-narrative-early-warning-prior-art.md §3

## What it was cited for

The German federal information-security agency's account of the Doppelgänger campaign, describing its objectives as reducing international support for Ukraine, strengthening pro-Russian positions, and shaping opinion ahead of elections in the United States and other countries including Germany, through counterfeit websites impersonating established media brands.

Cited as the official-source corroboration for the campaign's existence, methods and German targeting, alongside the EU DisinfoLab resource. Related reporting notes that the German non-profit CeMAS detected Doppelgänger-linked activity around the 2025 German federal election, which is the closest analogue to the DACH election context our project would eventually serve.
