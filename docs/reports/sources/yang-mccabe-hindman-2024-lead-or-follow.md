# Does Russian Propaganda Lead or Follow? Topic Coverage, User Engagement, and RT and Sputnik's Agenda Influence on US Media

- **Publisher / venue:** Yun-Kang Yang, Stefan D. McCabe, Matthew Hindman — The International Journal of Press/Politics (2024)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-26

## What it was cited for

A large-scale test of whether Russian state media set or follow the Western news agenda. The authors assembled 4.7 million English-language articles from RT, Sputnik and 67 other outlets that were shared on Facebook between 2017 and 2021, built topic-coverage time series, and ran Granger-causality tests with impulse-response functions. Their conclusion is conditional rather than uniform: on the geopolitical subjects the Russian outlets prioritise, those outlets' coverage statistically precedes coverage by US media of every political leaning, including centrist mainstream outlets; on US domestic subjects the direction reverses and the Russian outlets follow, with right-wing and far-right US outlets sometimes leading them instead.

This is the single most important source for our project because it tests our original hypothesis directly. It supports a lead effect only for geopolitics, and our chosen case studies are domestic. Scope limits to keep in mind: US media only, English only, 2017–2021, and the unit of analysis is topic coverage rather than specific claims.
