## What it was cited for

The mechanism by which vetted researchers can obtain non-public data from very large online platforms. The delegated act specifying the procedure was adopted in July 2025 and the centralised Data Access Portal became available on 28 October 2025. Requests go through Digital Services Coordinators, with notification timelines of roughly 80 working days as specified in Article 7 of the delegated act, and the first decisions were expected in late February 2026. Commentary and early experience reports describe implementation as inconsistent across member states and narrow in scope, with researchers refused broad access, or any access, on privacy and confidentiality grounds.

Cited to settle a scoping question. Platform data is a genuine route to the social-media layer, but on a multi-month institutional timeline with a real chance of refusal, so it belongs in deferred work rather than in a hackathon plan, and the prototype must be designed to produce results without it.
