## What it was cited for

A shared task providing a framework for studying how online news constructs and spreads manipulative narratives, split into three subtasks: entity framing, which identifies the roles entities play within a narrative; narrative classification, which assigns documents fine-grained labels from a topic-specific narrative taxonomy; and narrative extraction, which produces a justification for a document's dominant narrative. The material covers two domains, the Ukraine-Russia war and climate change, in Bulgarian, English, Hindi, Portuguese and Russian.

Cited for two reasons. The published narrative taxonomy and the three-way task decomposition give us a defensible, externally validated formalism instead of an invented one, and the absence of German from the language set marks a concrete gap a German-language contribution could fill.
