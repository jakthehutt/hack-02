A roundup of the narrative-intelligence field used here for short descriptions of the other vendors: Alethea, whose Artemis AI platform is described as delivering early warning of narrative threats along with analysis of who is behind activity, how it spreads and whether it is coordinated, plus threat investigation, executive protection, synthetic-media detection and mitigation workflows; Graphika, which maps and analyses online networks to trace information flow across platforms; and Logically, which offers narrative decision intelligence through Logically Intelligence and PRISMα.
